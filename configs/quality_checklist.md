# Quality Checklist

A good report must satisfy all of the following:

## Fidelity
- All major claims are supported by the paper
- No fabricated figure/table references
- No invented experimental values

## Technical Depth
- Inputs and outputs are clearly described
- Pipeline is explained step by step
- Core modules are explained
- Training and inference are explained
- Novelty is stated precisely
- Important technical terms are explained on first mention

## Experimental Rigor
- Main benchmark/dataset is identified
- Metrics are identified
- Key result table is included in Markdown
- Important ablations are summarized

## Writing Quality
- Fluent and natural
- Scientifically rigorous
- No vague hype words
- No unnecessary repetition
- No repetitive mechanical prefixes such as "作者明确表述"
- Source attribution is integrated naturally into sentences
- Main Technology is not shallower than the experiments section

## Uncertainty
- Missing or ambiguous details are explicitly marked
- Interpretation is separated from author claims
